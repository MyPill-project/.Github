# 💊 MyPill

**The right pill, at the right time, with the people who care kept in the loop.**

MyPill is a smart pill dispenser and companion app that gives out medication on
schedule, makes sure it was taken, and lets family or a nurse know when a dose is missed.

## Why MyPill

Taking several medications a day is hard to get right, especially for older
people, patients just home from hospital, and anyone on a long-term treatment.
Doses get skipped, taken twice, or taken late. And the people who support them,
a daughter, a neighbour, a home nurse, often only find out once something has
already gone wrong.

MyPill takes the worry out of it, for the patient and for everyone around them.

## How it works

1. **Set up the week once.** The patient or a caregiver enters the medication
   schedule in the app. It repeats every week until someone changes it.
2. **MyPill hands out the pills.** At each dose time, the dispenser serves the
   right pills, ready to take. Nothing to press, nothing to remember.
3. **MyPill checks they were taken.** No need to tick anything off: the
   dispenser notices on its own.
4. **The right people are told.** The patient gets a reminder when it's time.
   Caregivers are alerted only if a dose is missed, so no news is good news.

## Features

- 🕗 **Automatic dispensing** of several medications, at the right time, every day
- ✅ **Intake check** with no button to press
- 🔔 **Reminders** for the patient, and **missed-dose alerts** for caregivers
- 👪 **A support circle**: family, friends and nurses can follow a loved one,
  and stay informed when the schedule changes
- 📈 **A clear history** of every dose, taken or missed
- 🔋 **At a glance** in the app: battery level and pills left
- 📶 **Keeps working** even when the internet is down
- 🔌 **Set up in minutes** from your phone, then it runs on its own

## Who it's for

- **Older people** who want to stay independent at home
- **Patients back from hospital** with a new or complex treatment
- **Families and caregivers** who want peace of mind without calling every day
- **Home-care nurses and care homes** following several patients

## Your data, protected

- **You decide who follows you.** Nobody can link to a patient without the
  patient's permission.
- **Hosted in France**, by a provider certified for health data, in line with GDPR.
